Quam Olim

Quam olim Abrahae promisisti, et semini eius — “as once you promised to Abraham and his seed” — is the fugue that closes the Offertorium of Mozart's Requiem; both the Domine Jesu and the Hostias end on it. The film takes its name from that promise.

The Lacrimosa is the movement Mozart was writing when he died in December 1791. He completed only its first eight bars; Franz Xaver Süssmayr finished it.
